\subsection{Dataset and Setup}
We evaluate our pipeline on a 21-second basketball sequence (525 frames, 25\,fps) captured by three synchronized cameras (cam\_13, cam\_2, cam\_4). 
Camera intrinsics are provided, while extrinsics are estimated via 2D-3D point correspondences of known court landmarks on the first frame, 
modeling lens distortion directly within the pose solver. 
For evaluation, ground-truth bounding boxes are annotated every fifth frame.
