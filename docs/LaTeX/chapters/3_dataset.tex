\section{Dataset and Experimental Setup}\label{sec:dataset}

Experiments are conducted on a basketball recording from the Sanbapolis sports
facility in Trento, Italy.
Three synchronized cameras (cam\_13, cam\_2, and cam\_4) capture a single game act,
each producing 525 frames at 25\,fps (approximately 21 seconds).
The cameras provide complementary viewpoints of the court, enabling multi-view
triangulation.

Ground-truth bounding-box annotations are available for every fifth frame (105
annotated frames per camera), yielding 1408, 544, and 1143 annotated instances for
cam\_13, cam\_2, and cam\_4 respectively.
All detection and tracking metrics are computed exclusively on these annotated
frames, so no predicted detections on unannotated frames are counted as false
positives.

Camera intrinsic parameters (focal length, principal point, and radial distortion
coefficients) are provided directly.
Extrinsic parameters are computed by clicking known court landmarks on the first
frame and pairing the selected image coordinates with their fixed 3D positions in
the court coordinate system; lens distortion is modelled within the pose solver
without pre-undistorting the image.
